What is Simply by Daniel?

Simply by Daniel is a brand and website design practice in Minnesota. It makes logos, brand kits and websites for companies that want one clear identity.

What does Simply design?

Logos, brand kits (colours, type and usage rules) and websites.

How does the process work?

Six steps: Words, Research, Sketch, Digital design, Finish and System. A website follows when it is part of the project.

Where is Simply based?

Minnesota. Projects from anywhere are welcome.

Can I see an example?

Yes. Simply × Briks shows a full brand and website, step by step.

What is the free brand audit?

A free one-minute quiz. Eight questions about how your business shows up online give you a score and a fix list. I will also record a short walkthrough of your brand and site.

How long does a brand and website take?

A brand and website built together are delivered within 2 weeks.

How do I start a project?

Send a message with the contact form.
