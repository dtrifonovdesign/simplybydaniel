Privacy policy

Last updated October 9, 2026

This site, simplybydaniel.com, is run by Daniel as Simply by Daniel. This page explains, in plain language, what the site collects and why. The short version: very little, and nothing is sold or used for advertising.

What you send me

If you use the contact form, I receive your email address and the message you write. I use them only to reply to you and to talk about a possible project. The form is delivered by a service called FormSubmit, which passes the message to my inbox, so it processes your message in order to deliver it. See formsubmit.co for how it handles data.

I keep enquiries for as long as they are useful, for example while we are talking or working together, and delete them on request.

Visit statistics

I use a privacy-friendly analytics service (Umami) to see how many people visit, which sections they reach and whether they send the form. It does not use cookies, does not follow you across other sites, and does not store your IP address or any information that identifies you. It also respects your browser's Do Not Track setting.

Cookies and storage

This site sets no advertising or tracking cookies. It saves two small items in your browser's local storage so it works well and does not repeat itself:

  • a display setting (simply-tier) that remembers if your device needed a lighter version of the animations;
  • a note (simply-notice) that you have seen the privacy notice.

Neither item contains personal information, and neither leaves your device. You can clear them any time in your browser settings.

Other services

  • Google Fonts serves this site's typefaces, so your browser contacts Google to download them, and Google receives your IP address as part of that request.
  • Cloudflare manages the domain name, and GitHub Pages hosts the site. Like any web host, they process technical request data such as your IP address to deliver the pages.

Your choices

You can ask me what I hold about you, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it. Use the contact form on the home page and say what you need. You can also write to your local data protection authority if you think I handled your data badly.

Changes

If this policy changes, I will update the date at the top of this page.
