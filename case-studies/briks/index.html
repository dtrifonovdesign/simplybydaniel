    The challenge

    Pavel has been laying floors in Minnesota for ten years, and people love his work. But his logo came from AI, and it didn’t look like him.

    Before

    01Words

    I start with a questionnaire: plain words and sliders, no design jargon.

    What I heard: modern look, classic quality, exclusive.

    Words that feel like your company.

      Nothing playful. Nothing handmade.

      Where does your brand sit?

      Classic, serious, minimal, soft. All the way to exclusive.

      02Research

      I study similar brands and map the range of premium, from old money to super modern.

      03Sketch

      Logos start on paper. The strongest idea: a tall plank with tiles stepping out to form a B.
