    The Simply Process

    Six steps, one path. Follow it, or jump to any stop.

    01Words

    Before any brief even begins, I ask for some words to describe the company. Those words then help form the brief.

    CalmCraftedTrustworthyWarmPremiumClear

    02Research

    I look at similar brands and see what elements could work well with the project. Those elements eventually come to form your brand, along with my personal flair.

    Three brands with a similar feel

    03Sketch

    Logos get drawn by hand, dozens of them. The best ideas are usually the simplest ones, and they show up on paper before they show up on a screen.

    Rough shapesTwo halvesThe gap decidedSoft corners

    04Digital design

    I use my sketches to refine the design digitally. Exact geometry, with better mock-ups.

    Exact curves8-unit gapSoft cornersAnchor points

    05Finish

    The final marks get cleaned up and exported in every format a brand needs: color, reversed, one-color and app icon.

    06System

    Colors, typefaces and one clear message. A tagline only if the brand needs one.

    About

    Hi, I'm Daniel.

    I'm a brand designer who builds identities by hand first and by machine second.

    My projects promise attention-to-detail and purpose in every step of the design process. When I was ten, I started helping my mom out with her websites and her business cards. That passion grew into something further, where I learned real design skills, platforms, and what it means to hold a brand.

    I've talked to business leaders and researched hundreds of websites and businesses. That led me to form Simply.

    My Rules:

    • By hand first.
    • Led by you.
    • One clear brand identity.
