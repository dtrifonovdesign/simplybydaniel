    About

    Hi, I'm Daniel.

    I'm a brand designer who builds identities by hand first and by machine second.

    I care about the quiet details: a curve drawn on purpose, a gap that earns its place, a color used once instead of everywhere. Every brand I make starts as words and pencil sketches, and it gets rebuilt carefully before it gets finished.

    You can see the whole process above. It is the same every time: words first, then paper, then precise geometry.

    • By hand first. Paper before pixels.
    • Words before shapes. The brief comes from you.
